# PRISM Decision Register

Generated: 2026-07-28

## Binding decisions

1. **Forward evidence only for promotion.** V2.2 may be promoted only through genuine pre-match frozen predictions paired later with verified outcomes.
2. **Predictions are immutable after freeze.** No result-aware editing, replacement, cherry-picking, or retrospective reconstruction.
3. **Home/Away Regime has high weight.** Season aggregate form must not erase venue-specific structure.
4. **Competitive/Season Readiness is independent.** Official-season readiness, preseason status, fixture rhythm, and competitive state must be modeled separately.
5. **Market Double Counting Control.** 1X2, Asian handicap and favourite status are not independent evidence streams; market information is used once as latent-strength calibration.
6. **Projected Event != Confirmed Event.** Expected rotation, injuries or tactical changes are probabilistic scenarios, not 100% lambda adjustments.
7. **Personnel/Team Regime Breaks matter.** Major transfers, returns, registration constraints and structural personnel changes may invalidate naive historical continuity.
8. **Dual-score output remains the formal exact-score interface.** Primary and secondary scorelines are frozen together.
9. **Governed promotion is machine-enforced.** PROMOTE exits 0, HOLD exits 2, REJECT exits 3; CI must preserve and enforce this contract.
10. **Promotion artifacts are auditable.** They carry schema version, policy, reasons, release-gate status and exact ledger provenance/fingerprints.
11. **GitHub is the source of truth for durable PRISM project memory.** Handoff documents are regenerated before changing chat contexts.
12. **Current live samples are frozen.** Rosenborg-Fredrikstad (2-1 / 2-0) and Hacken-AIK (2-1 / 1-1) are forward-test evidence and cannot be revised after outcomes are known.
